2. Calibrated Risk Models & Loss Forecasting (2026 Update)

Actuarial Calibration Results

Uncalibrated cost-sensitive ensembles systematically overestimate claim probability, inflating customer premiums. We apply 5-fold cross-validated Isotonic Regression Calibration:

  • Uncalibrated Ensemble: Brier = 0.2265, ECE = 0.1348
  • Isotonic Calibrated: Brier = 0.2072, ECE = 0.0182, ROC-AUC = 0.725
  • Portfolio Loss Forecast: Predicted $1,376,917 vs. Actual $1,375,447 (99.89% accuracy).

3. Fairness Constraints & Socioeconomic Bias Mitigation

Satisfying the 80% Disparate Impact Rule

Historical credit stability scores carry socioeconomic proxy bias against Low-Income Zone applicants. Our two-stage fairness constraint applies Kamiran-Calders sample weights during training and group parity calibration at inference:

  • Disparate Impact Ratio (Before): 0.6171 (Fails ≥0.80 threshold)
  • Disparate Impact Ratio (After): 0.9917 (Passes regulatory threshold)
  • Statistical Parity Difference: Improved from -0.0778 to -0.0039

4. Data Privacy & Differential Privacy (ε-Laplace)

Privacy-Preserving Telemetry & PII Protection

  • Salted SHA-256 Tokenization: Converts raw applicant names into irreversible tokens (TOK-8F91A2B4C1E0).
  • National ID Redaction: Masks identifiers to ID-XXXX-3910.
  • k-Anonymity Generalization: Buckets exact ages into actuarial bands.
  • ε-Differential Privacy: Injects calibrated Laplace noise Lap(0, Δf/ε) into sensitive credit and telematics scores. At ε=2.5, full calibration utility is preserved.

5. Continuous Model Monitoring & Drift Detection (PSI & KS)

Quarterly Production Drift Surveillance

Monitors every feature using the Population Stability Index (PSI) and two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) tests:

  • 2026-Q1 & Q2: Max PSI < 0.10 → HEALTHY
  • 2026-Q3 (Mild Telematics Shift): Max PSI = 0.4182 → WATCH ALERT
  • 2026-Q4 (Severe Weather & Macro Shift): Max PSI = 3.0132 → CRITICAL: AUTO-RECALIBRATE
